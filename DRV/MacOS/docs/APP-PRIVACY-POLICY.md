# Time Card Control Center privacy policy

Effective September 7, 2026.

Time Card Control Center is a free macOS application. This policy describes
the native macOS app and its included Time Card driver.

## Local hardware information

Time Card Control Center reads information from Time Card hardware, attached
GNSS receivers, atomic clocks, motion sensors and serial devices to provide
the functions you use. This may include precise GNSS coordinates, device serial
numbers, timing measurements, sensor readings and the contents of serial
messages. The app processes this information on your Mac.

The app does not require a user account and does not include advertising,
cross-app tracking, usage analytics or a custom automatic crash-upload service.
It does not automatically send your hardware readings or imported files to
the developer or a remote service.

## Saved settings and files

The app saves preferences and configuration profiles locally. In the App Store
configuration, its private settings and profile library reside in the app's
sandbox. Receiver captures and diagnostic records remain in the running app
unless you export them. Exported files persist at the destination you choose.

You can review and remove profiles through the profile library, change relevant
preferences in the app and remove exported files using Finder. Removing the
app does not necessarily remove previously exported files or all stored settings.

## Exports and support

Exports and support bundles are created only when you choose to save them.
Creating a support bundle does not upload it. Before sharing a file, inspect
it for precise location, hardware identifiers, private serial messages and
other information you do not want to disclose.

If you save to a cloud-synchronized folder or share a file through another
service, that service handles it under its own policies and your settings.
A public issue tracker is not a private support channel.

If you email support, the recipient receives your email address, message and
any attachments you choose to send so that your request can be addressed.
This is a separate, voluntary communication, not automatic data collection
by the app. Email providers process those communications under their own
policies. Do not send credentials or unrelated personal information.

## macOS and this website

macOS controls driver approval, sandbox access, system logs, backups and any
operating-system crash reporting. These services are governed by your system
settings and Apple's applicable policies. The app's own driver records limited
startup and error messages in the local macOS system log.

This policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub. Visiting GitHub is
subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The app does not send its hardware data to GitHub automatically.
