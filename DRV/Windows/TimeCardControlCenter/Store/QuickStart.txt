TimeCardControlCenter
Author: Ahmad Byagowi

Open the app normally to explore its interface and diagnostics. Enable Demo in
the sidebar to view clearly labeled synthetic data without timing hardware.

For live hardware, install a supported PCIe Time Card and its compatible
Microsoft-signed Time Card driver separately. The app requires driver ABI 15.
Select Restart as administrator and approve Windows User Account Control,
then select a detected card. Each card has its own workspace. Supported
features depend on the board, firmware, peripherals, and driver capabilities.

The app initially reads status. Clock adjustments, output routing, GNSS
configuration, and firmware operations change hardware state. Review the
selected card and the operation before applying a change. Firmware operations
require their own explicit confirmation. A GNSS antenna and suitable reception
are required for satellite synchronization.

Diagnostic reports are exported only when requested. The default destination
is Documents. Reports may contain device identifiers or GNSS position data;
review them before sharing. PrivacyPolicy.txt describes local data handling
and the optional user-configured oscillatord service connection.
