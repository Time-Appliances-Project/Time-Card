Name: TimeCardControlCenter
Suggested category: Developer tools
Author: Ahmad Byagowi
Support contact: clk@wiwistamp.com

Short description
Monitor and manage your Time Card hardware from Windows.

Description
TimeCardControlCenter brings Time Card clock readings, hardware status, GNSS information, supported timing interfaces, and engineering diagnostics into one Windows dashboard.

Choose a connected card, inspect its precision hardware clock, compare it with Windows time, explore available subsystems, and record or export local diagnostic data. Multiple connected cards retain separate identities and workspaces. A synthetic demo is available without hardware.

Features depend on the card model, FPGA firmware, connected instruments, and installed driver. The application supports the Time Card driver ABI 15, including WiWi revision 02, Meta, Celestica, and Orolia/Safran ART profiles. Not every board exposes the same registers or peripherals.

Requirements
Windows 10 version 2004 or later, x64; a supported Time Card PCIe device and a separately installed compatible driver for live hardware use. Administrator approval is required to access the hardware driver. GNSS reception requires an appropriate antenna and reception conditions. Demo mode does not require a card.
